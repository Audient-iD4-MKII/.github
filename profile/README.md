## What is Audient iD4?

**Audient iD4** is a highly acclaimed professional USB audio interface, renowned for delivering studio-quality sound to home studios, podcasters, and musicians. Building on Audient's legacy of console-grade circuitry, the iD4 provides pristine microphone preamps, high-resolution converters, and robust connectivity in a compact, desktop-friendly form factor.

The Audient iD4 is designed for creators who demand exceptional audio fidelity without complexity. It features a single Class-A mic preamp derived from Audient's renowned console designs, ensuring your recordings capture every detail with clarity and warmth. The high-headroom instrument input is perfect for recording guitars and basses directly, while the pristine monitor outputs deliver accurate sound for critical mixing and listening.

Whether you're recording vocals, instruments, podcasts, or streaming, the iD4 offers a seamless and professional workflow. Its dedicated hardware controls, including a smart rotary encoder for monitor volume and a unique "Scroll" function for navigating your DAW, make it an intuitive centerpiece for any creative space. The included software bundle, often featuring licenses for popular DAWs and plugins, adds significant value, making the iD4 an complete audio solution.

## 📚 Quick Start Guide

1. Connect the **Audient iD4** to your computer via USB-C cable.
2. Install any recommended drivers from the Audient website (if required).
3. Connect your microphone, instrument, and studio monitors/headphones.
4. Set the iD4 as your audio input/output device in your computer's settings.
5. Open your DAW and start recording with studio-quality sound.
